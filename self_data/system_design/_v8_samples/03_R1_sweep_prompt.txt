- **`amount_remaining`** — fill level of the stock container AFTER dispensing (post-dispense view, put-back / storage-return frame, or the last sighting if the package disappears late). You are not forced to pick one — fill any combination of the three amount fields that the frames support. They are independent measurements. If `status == "not_used"`, all three amounts should typically be `null` (or just `amount_remaining` reflecting an unchanged package fill — your call).

"Remaining" / "starting" reads count only what is still inside the stock container; loose portions on plates / bowls / pans / cutting boards count toward `amount_derivative`, not the others.
